Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who uses this app

WorkBuddy Calendar is a personal tool used only by its owner (ravenlin23@gmail.com) to connect their own Google Calendar to their AI assistant. It is not offered to other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app accesses Google Calendar data (calendars and events) through the Google Calendar API, only to carry out requests the owner makes, such as listing, creating, or updating events.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app does not operate any server and does not store calendar data elsewhere.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
